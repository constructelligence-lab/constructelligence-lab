## Constructelligence

**Construction intelligence for contractors** — job cost forecasting, early warnings and analytics built on
the data a construction company already has. Our forecasting technology and models are in private beta at
[constructelligence.co](https://constructelligence.co).

We also open source the data, prompts, skills and tools we use along the way. Everything below is free to use,
CC BY 4.0 unless stated, and written for people who actually run jobs.

### The guides

| [**ai-in-construction**](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/ai-in-construction) | A practical guide to AI in construction: what works today, what your data has to look like, the risks, and a 90-day plan. |
| [**construction-data-migration**](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-data-migration) | Moving a construction company between systems without losing a cent: a guide, templates and a reconciliation toolkit. |

### Records and reference data

| Repository | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [**construction-project-records**](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-project-records) | Open schemas, templates and a checker for RFIs, submittals, change events, daily reports and punch lists. |
| [**construction-data**](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-data) | Construction reference data in CSV: cost codes, CSI divisions, units, waste factors, pay units, trade sequence, glossary, metric formulas. |
| [**construction-mcp**](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-mcp) | An offline MCP server that gives AI assistants construction reference data and calculators. |

### Where to start

- **Owners and GMs weighing AI:** [the 90-day plan](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/ai-in-construction/blob/main/05-a-90-day-plan.md).
- **Estimators and PMs:** [construction prompts](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-prompts) or, in Claude Code, the [agent skills](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-agent-skills).
- **Controllers and PMs moving systems:** [construction data migration](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-data-migration).
- **Setting up cost codes or a data model:** [construction-data](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-data).
- **Building your own tools:** [open source construction tools](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/open-source-construction-tools) and the [MCP server](https://github.com/constructelligence-lab/construction-mcp).

Contributions and corrections are welcome as issues or pull requests on any repository.
